2. What ThriftSnap AI Does

ThriftSnap AI lets you photograph clothing items, uses artificial intelligence to attempt to identify them (brand, style, era), and provides an estimated resale value range. Estimates draw on publicly available marketplace listings and the AI's own general knowledge of resale values.

3. Estimates Are Not Guarantees

  • All valuations shown in the App are estimates only. They are generated automatically from AI identification and current marketplace listing prices, and are provided for informational purposes only.
  • Estimates are not appraisals, not offers to buy, and not a guarantee that any item can be sold at any particular price.
  • AI identification can be wrong. Item condition, authenticity, and market demand — which materially affect real-world value — cannot be reliably determined from a photograph.
  • Estimates vary in how they are produced, and the App labels which method was used. Some are calculated from current listings; some are the AI's own estimate where no comparable listings were found, which is a recollection rather than observed market data. Where two methods disagree the App shows both rather than choosing for you.
  • Listing-based estimates reflect what sellers are asking, which is not necessarily what items sell for.
  • You agree not to rely on the App's estimates for financial, insurance, investment, or resale decisions without independent verification.

ThriftSnap — Privacy Policy

⚠️ DRAFT — NOT LEGAL ADVICE. This is a starting draft generated to cover ThriftSnap's core features. Have it reviewed by a qualified attorney before publishing the app — privacy policies are legally required and regulated (e.g. CCPA, GDPR if you have EU users, and Apple's App Store requirements).

Last updated: July 9, 2026

1. What We Collect

Photos you take in the App. When you scan an item, your photo is uploaded to our servers (hosted on Supabase) and sent to a third-party AI service (Anthropic's Claude) to identify the item. Photos are stored so your scan history and closet work across devices.

Account information. If you create an account: your email address and a hashed password (we never store your password in plain text).

Scan and closet data. Item identifications, price estimates, what you tell us you paid for items, and items you save to your closet.

Subscription status. Whether you have an active subscription, so we can apply the correct scan limits. Payment details (credit card, billing address) are handled entirely by Apple — we never see or store them.

Usage limits data. A counter of scans used per month (stored on your device for anonymous use; in your account once signed in).

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide the App's core features: identifying items, estimating values, and maintaining your closet.
  • To enforce free-tier usage limits.
  • To operate, debug, and improve the App.

We do not sell your personal data or use it for third-party advertising.

3. Third-Party Services We Share Data With

Service What is shared Why
Supabase Photos, account info, scan/closet data Database, storage, and login infrastructure
Anthropic (Claude AI) Your item photos AI identification of clothing items
eBay (Browse API) Text search queries derived from your scans (never your photos or identity) Finding comparable listings for price estimates
Apple Subscription purchases Payment processing

Each of these providers has its own privacy policy governing their handling of data.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Scan photos and closet data are retained while your account is active.
  • You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at justin.ashkar@outlook.com, and we will delete it within 30 days.
  • Anonymous scan photos may be retained for service operation and debugging.

5. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with access controls (including database row-level security so only you can access your own closet and scan history). No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Children's Privacy

ThriftSnap is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to object to certain processing. Contact us at justin.ashkar@outlook.com to exercise these rights.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated in the App or by email.

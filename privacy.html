ThriftSnap — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

1. What We Collect

Photos you take in the App. When you scan an item you take a photo of the item, and optionally a second close-up of its brand or size tag. Both are uploaded to our servers (hosted on Supabase) and sent to a third-party AI service (Anthropic's Claude) to identify the item. Photos are stored so your scan history and closet work across devices. Images are resized on your device before upload.

Profile information. If you add them: a display name and a profile photo. Both are optional — the App works without either.

Account information. If you create an account: your email address and a hashed password (we never store your password in plain text).

Scan and closet data. Item identifications, price estimates, what you tell us you paid for items, and items you save to your closet.

Subscription status. Whether you have an active subscription, when the current paid period ends, and how many scans you have used, so we can apply the correct limits. Payment details (credit card, billing address) are handled entirely by Apple — we never see or store them. Subscriptions are managed through RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous account identifier and your purchase status so the App knows what to unlock.

Preferences. Your chosen display currency, stored on your device.

Your IP address, for abuse prevention only. When you scan, we record your IP address against a counter so we can limit how many scans can be made from one device or network in a given hour or day. Without this, anyone could script unlimited scans against our AI provider at our expense. These counters are used for nothing else — not analytics, not advertising, not profiling — are never linked to your account or your photos, and are deleted after 7 days.

Technical usage records. For each scan we record how much AI processing it consumed and what it cost us, so we can understand and control the cost of running the service. This holds no photo and no content of your item.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide the App's core features: identifying items, estimating values, and maintaining your closet.
  • To enforce free-tier usage limits.
  • To prevent abuse — rate limiting how often scans can be requested, so automated misuse cannot run up costs or degrade the service for others.
  • To operate, debug, and improve the App.

We do not sell your personal data or use it for third-party advertising.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Items you save to your closet are retained while your account is active.
  • Scans you do not save to your closet are deleted automatically, along with their photos, once they fall outside your recent scan history.
  • If your Pro subscription ends and your closet holds more items than the free tier allows, you choose which items to keep. Items you do not keep are permanently deleted.
  • Replacing your profile photo deletes the previous one.
  • You can delete your account at any time from Settings in the app. This permanently removes your profile, closet, scan history, scan photos and profile photo. You may also request deletion by contacting us at justin.ashkar@outlook.com, and we will delete your data within 30 days.
  • Scans taken without an account are not linked to you, and are deleted automatically, along with their photos, 14 days after they are made.
  • Abuse-prevention records containing IP addresses are deleted after 7 days.

5. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your account data — closet, scan history, profile and subscription status — is protected by database row-level security, so only you can read or change your own records.

Please note how photos are stored. Uploaded photos (scan images and profile pictures) are held in public storage buckets. They are not listed anywhere and their web addresses contain long random identifiers that are impractical to guess, but they are not individually access-controlled: anyone who obtains a photo's exact URL can view it without signing in. Treat scanning as you would posting a photo to an unlisted link, and avoid capturing anything in frame you would not want seen that way.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Children's Privacy

ThriftSnap is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to object to certain processing. Contact us at justin.ashkar@outlook.com to exercise these rights.

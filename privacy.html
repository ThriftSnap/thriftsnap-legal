ThriftSnap — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

This policy covers ThriftSnap: AI Resale Value (the "App"), listed under that name on the Apple App Store and referred to throughout as ThriftSnap.

1. What We Collect

Photos you take in the App. When you scan an item you take a photo of the item, and optionally a second close-up of its brand or size tag. Both are uploaded to our servers (hosted on Supabase) and sent to a third-party AI service (Anthropic's Claude) to identify the item. Photos are stored so your scan history and closet work across devices. Images are resized on your device before upload.

Profile information. If you add them: a display name and a profile photo. Both are optional — the App works without either.

Account information. If you create an account: your email address and a hashed password (we never store your password in plain text).

Scan and closet data. Item identifications, price estimates, items you save to your closet, and the amounts you choose to enter: what you paid for an item and, if you mark it sold, what you sold it for.

Subscription status. Whether you have an active subscription, which plan you are on, when the current paid period ends, and how many scans you have used, so we can apply the correct limits. Payment details (credit card, billing address) are handled entirely by Apple — we never see or store them. Subscriptions are managed through RevenueCat, which receives your purchase status and your account's internal identifier. That identifier is a random code rather than your name or email, but it does identify your account to us, so we describe it as pseudonymous rather than anonymous.

Preferences. Your chosen display currency, stored on your device.

Your IP address, for abuse prevention only, and only in one case. Scanning in the App requires an account, and for a signed-in scan the rate-limit counter is keyed to your account — no IP address is recorded at all. An IP is recorded only where a scan is requested without an account, which the App itself never does but our interface permits; without that limit anyone could script unlimited scans against our AI provider at our expense. Where an IP is recorded it is used for nothing else — not analytics, not advertising, not profiling — is never linked to an account or to any photo, and is deleted after 7 days.

Technical usage records. For each scan we record how much AI processing it consumed and what it cost us, so we can understand and control the cost of running the service. This holds no photo and no content of your item. If you delete your account these records are kept, but the link to your account is removed, so what remains is an anonymous cost figure that cannot be traced back to you.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide the App's core features: identifying items, estimating values, and maintaining your closet.
  • To enforce free-tier usage limits.
  • To prevent abuse — rate limiting how often scans can be requested, so automated misuse cannot run up costs or degrade the service for others.
  • To operate, debug, and improve the App.

We do not sell your personal data or use it for third-party advertising.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Items you save to your closet are retained while your account is active.
  • Scans you do not save to your closet are deleted automatically, along with their photos, once they fall outside your recent scan history.
  • If your Pro subscription ends and your closet holds more items than the free tier allows, you choose which items to keep. Items you do not keep are permanently deleted.
  • Replacing your profile photo deletes the previous one.
  • You can delete your account at any time from Settings in the app. This permanently removes your profile, closet, scan history, scan photos and profile photo. You may also request deletion by contacting us at thriftsnapai@gmail.com, and we will delete your data within 30 days.
  • Scans taken without an account are not linked to you, and are deleted automatically, along with their photos, 14 days after they are made.
  • Abuse-prevention records are deleted after 7 days. Those recorded against a signed-in account contain no IP address; see section 1.
  • Technical usage records (what a scan cost us to run) are retained, but on account deletion the link to your account is removed and only an anonymous cost figure remains.

5. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your account data — closet, scan history, profile and subscription status — is protected by database row-level security, so only you can read or change your own records.

Please note how photos are stored. Uploaded photos (scan images and profile pictures) are held in public storage buckets. They are not listed anywhere and their web addresses contain long random identifiers that are impractical to guess, but they are not individually access-controlled: anyone who obtains a photo's exact URL can view it without signing in. Treat scanning as you would posting a photo to an unlisted link, and avoid capturing anything in frame you would not want seen that way.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Children's Privacy

ThriftSnap is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
